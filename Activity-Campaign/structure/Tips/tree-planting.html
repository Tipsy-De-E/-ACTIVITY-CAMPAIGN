Tree-Planting Activity

Project: Plant Today, Prepare for Tomorrow — Execution Framework

Activity Goal

To encourage students, faculty, and local residents to participate directly in tree planting while learning actionable environmental preparedness strategies for El Niño resilience.

Execution Steps

1. Before the Activity

• Select an ideal planting zone in coordination with barangay/school leaders.
• Source indigenous seedling species suited for dry resistance.
• Gather shovels, organic fertilizer, mulching material, and water containers.
• Brief all participants on safety protocols and correct sapling handling.

2. During the Activity

• Gather participants for a short orientation on El Niño awareness.
• Demonstrate proper hole depth and root placement techniques.
• Carefully plant seedlings, tamp down soil, and apply mulch.
• Attach plant tags/labels and thoroughly document with photos and videos.

3. After the Activity

• Establish a watering schedule using recycled or harvested water.
• Clear competing weeds from around sapling stems.
• Monitor survival rates and record growth metrics monthly.
• Maintain active community ownership over tree care.
