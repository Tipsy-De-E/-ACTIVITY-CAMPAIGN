Role of TCM Students

El Niño can bring hotter and drier conditions that severely affect communities. As Transcultural Communication & Media (TCM) students, we utilize strategic communication, digital technology, and grassroots activism to spread vital environmental awareness and encourage proactive actions that help our communities build lasting climate resilience.

Plant a Tree

Trees provide vital shade, lower ambient temperatures in built-up areas, and enhance soil health during extended dry spells.

Spread Awareness

Empower families and neighbors with practical, actionable information on water preservation and extreme heat safety.

Build Preparedness

Unite community leaders, students, and citizens to minimize heat-related risks through joint environmental initiatives.
