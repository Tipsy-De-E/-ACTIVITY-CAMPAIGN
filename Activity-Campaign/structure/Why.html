Why Tree Planting?

How trees act as direct mitigation allies during hot and dry El Niño cycles.

Note: While tree planting is not a complete single solution to El Niño, strategic vegetation contributes significantly to environmental resilience and community cooling.

Shade & Cooling

Tree canopies intercept direct sunlight, lowering surface temperatures by up to 10–20°C in shaded areas.

Soil Water Retention

Roots improve soil structure, enhancing water infiltration during rain and keeping subsoil moisture intact.

Local Microclimate Improvement

Evapotranspiration from leaves cools surrounding air, creating pleasant, greener neighborhood pockets.

Long-Term Climate Action

Establishes sustainable ecosystem support that buffers against future environmental shocks.
